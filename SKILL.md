## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one small soul-mark drawn as if from memory, not a polished logo. Use a rounded irregular form, a wobbling contour, a gap or two, and the grain of ink, pencil, wax pastel and gouache. Color is warm and low-stimulus: cream, mist blue, sage, soft orange, blush, warm brown. The icon hangs alone in a large blank, like a note in a private journal. Type is a tiny unstable editorial word. Reject a complex illustration, a commercial mascot, cute overload, smooth vector and 3D.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.
